UCL School of Management

Strategy by Design

MSIN0020

Instructor · Core undergraduate course · Instructor rating 4.93 / 5.00

Course description

Key approaches in strategic management, and how core concepts and frameworks can be applied to analyse the strategic development of organisations in uncertain, new, and rapidly changing environments. Sessions are built around case discussions, with recent editions also examining how AI is reshaping strategy and competitive advantage, and culminate in a final company case project.

Course description

An integrative course on strategy formulation and competitive advantage, taken from a general management perspective. Through case discussions, students analyse industries and competitors, the sources of sustainable advantage, and corporate and global strategy decisions, culminating in a comprehensive strategic analysis of a company.
